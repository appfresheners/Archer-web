Aim & Release

Features to add to Archer — the GTD Goal & Project Creator that turns fuzzy goals into concrete next actions.

Goal — explore the full landscape of possible features • 2026-08-31 • Facilitated by Mary, Business Analyst

Four techniques, run in sequence, drew a wide arc across the possible feature space — then collapsed it to a bullseye. Every idea below traces to a moment in the session. The synthesis at the end is where the arrows land.

Technique 01

SCAMPER Method — Seven Lenses on Archer

Turning the existing product over and viewing it through seven prompts: Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse. Each lens surfaced its own crop of features.

21 ideas
S
Substitute
swap a part of the flow
  • Voice input: speak your goal, get breakdown
  • Methodology selector: GTD / OKR / SMART / 12-week / Eisenhower
  • Rotating goal-of-the-day seeded from popular inputs
C
Combine
merge flows & outputs
  • Unified flow: expand any project inside a goal breakdown inline
  • Calendar .ics export so next actions become time-blocked events
  • Effort tags per action (2min / 15min / 1hr) to batch by energy
A
Adapt
borrow proven mechanics
  • Diff view: regenerate and show what changed vs last version
  • Spaced-repetition revisit reminder via generated calendar event (no account)
  • Time-to-done estimate for the whole project
M
Modify / Magnify
tune the intensity
  • Depth slider: quick 3 actions ⇄ exhaustive breakdown
  • GTD strictness toggle: enforce physical-verb-only actions
  • Why-this-matters motivational framing layer atop output
P
Put to Another Use
new audiences & jobs
  • Team mode: shared objective → role-split action lists
  • Meeting-prep tool: prepare for X → agenda + actions
  • Learning-path generator: learn X → curriculum breakdown
E
Eliminate
remove the friction
  • Auto-detect goal vs project, eliminate the mode toggle
  • Land on a pre-filled generated example, no blank page
  • One-click Open in Notion via template deep link
R
Reverse / Rearrange
flip the direction
  • Reverse: paste a messy brain-dump → organized GTD structure
  • Backcasting built-in: start from done, work backward to today
  • Archer asks clarifying GTD-coach questions, then generates
Technique 02

Job to Be Done — What People Actually Hire Archer For

Stepping past the feature list to ask what emotional and practical job a user pulls Archer in to do. The answer reframed everything that followed.

15 ideas
“

The reframe: Users don't want a GTD breakdown — they hire Archer for deeper emotional and practical jobs. Reduce overwhelm, find the one true first step, feel momentum, and know whether the goal is even worth it.

Cut the overwhelm

Just-tell-me-the-ONE-thing button: collapse to a single first action.

Emotional triage

Emotional check-in: “overwhelmed by X” → triage down to one tiny step.

Right now

Panic mode: strip everything to the next 10 minutes only.

Make it shareable

Export to a polished, shareable, read-only public page.

Present it

Present-this mode: turn the breakdown into slides.

Match the room

Professional vs casual tone toggle.

Keep me moving

Progress check-in: “how'd it go?” → regenerate the remaining actions.

Feel momentum

Local streak / momentum tracking, no account required.

Gentle nudges

Next-action-only email drip, opt-in and subscription-free.

Reality check

Feasibility gut-check: flag unrealistic goals for the timeframe.

Show the cost

Cost / tradeoff surfacing: estimated hours-per-week needed.

Should I even?

Should-I-even-do-this pre-mortem before planning begins.

Hide the method

Invisible methodology: never say “GTD”, just deliver clean actions.

Teach me

Beginner-mode inline explanations of GTD terms.

Speak my language

Auto-simplify jargon based on the detected domain.

◎ The Bullseye

Synthesis

Sixty-two arrows, four techniques — and they cluster around a handful of insights. This is where the session pays off.

🗝️
Keystone

Client-side memory unlocks a whole family of features

One idea — client-side memory (localStorage history / magic-link-QR identity / encrypted vault) — unlocks streaks, Wrapped, momentum, and check-ins, all while honoring the no-account / no-tracking NFR6.

🎯
Highest Leverage

Deepen the AI research layer

Bringing in real resources and current best-practice steps turns structure into intelligence — delivering directly on the brief's Vision.

🚀
Growth Engine

A Wordle-style shareable result card

Near-free to build, viral by design, and account-free distribution — growth that respects the product's constraints.

⚖️
Tension — Resolved

One slider serves both personas

Invisible-methodology (beginner) vs GTD-strictness (practitioner) resolves into a single depth / mode slider — serving both brief personas with one control.
