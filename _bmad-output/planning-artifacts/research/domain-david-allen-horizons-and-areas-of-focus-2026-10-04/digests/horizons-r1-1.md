# Digest: GTD Horizons and Areas of Focus

**Round:** 1, focused primary-source pass
**Accessed:** 2026-10-04
**Source quality:** Primary publisher is David Allen Company / official Getting Things Done site. Articles are dated 2010–2020, so they establish Allen's published model but may not reflect any later terminology updates. The current official overview was retrieved without a publication date.

## Claims

- **Horizon model:** David Allen Company describes six levels: Ground (calendar/actions); H1 Projects; H2 Areas of Focus and Accountability; H3 one- to two-year goals/objectives; H4 three- to five-year vision; H5 purpose and principles. The article says the altitude framing is an intentionally approximate but useful way to frame work. Source: [1], publisher David Allen Company, published 2011-01-26, accessed 2026-10-04, confidence high for the published framework.
- **Area/project distinction:** H2 areas are ongoing responsibilities and accountability, not outcomes to complete. The official article says reviewing areas can surface projects to create or eliminate. Source: [1], publisher David Allen Company, published 2011-01-26, accessed 2026-10-04, confidence high.
- **Project definition:** David Allen Company defines projects as outcomes requiring more than one action step; next actions are the immediate physical, visible actions, some project-related and some standalone. Source: [2], publisher David Allen Company, published 2010-02-15, accessed 2026-10-04, confidence high.
- **Review guidance:** The official weekly-review checklist says to review project and larger-outcome lists, evaluate projects/goals/outcomes one by one, and ensure a current next action for each. Get Creative includes reviewing Someday/Maybe. Source: [3] and [4], publisher David Allen Company, published 2018-08-15 and 2015-07-21 respectively, accessed 2026-10-04, confidence high.
- **No required app page:** The retrieved official material describes the conceptual model and review practice, but does not prescribe a dedicated Horizons page, navigation layout, or database model. This is a bounded search observation, not a claim that no such recommendation exists elsewhere.
- **Current overview:** The official GTD overview describes Capture, Clarify, Organize, Reflect, Engage and says to review pertinent system contents frequently; it does not detail horizon levels. Source: [5], publisher David Allen Company, publication date not shown, accessed 2026-10-04, confidence high for the visible overview text.
