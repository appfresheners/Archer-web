# Completion metrics and measurement context

Accessed 2026-10-13. Official service guidance is adapted as a useful metric-definition pattern, not treated as a binding standard for personal project software.

- **[claim]** A completion rate requires a defined start, end, completed numerator, and total-started denominator that includes partial/failed journeys. Saved-and-return journeys need starts matched to later completions; multiple end states must be enumerated. Source: Government Digital Service, “Measuring completion rate,” published 2016-03-31, updated 2021-02-19, https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/measuring-completion-rate (accessed 2026-10-13). Confidence: high for source content; adaptation to project portfolios is a design recommendation.
- **[claim]** Performance measures should be purpose-derived, have clear meaning, include a baseline, be measured over time rather than as isolated snapshots, and be interpreted with context/segments. Data should be checked for errors and analysis should answer what happened, why, and what action follows. Source: Government Digital Service, “How to set performance metrics for your service,” published 2017-12-05, https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/how-to-set-performance-metrics-for-your-service (accessed 2026-10-13). Confidence: high for official guidance; application is analogical.
- **[claim]** Measures for non-transactional end-to-end journeys can combine task completion and time, and repeated measurement can reveal whether tasks become easier. Source: Government Digital Service, “Measuring the success of your service,” published 2018-08-06, https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/measuring-the-success-of-your-service (accessed 2026-10-13). Confidence: high for guidance.

**Design inference:** Show distinct measures rather than an ambiguous combined “completion rate”: (a) portfolio state now (active/completed/paused/someday/archived counts); (b) completion of projects started within a selected cohort/time window; (c) throughput, completed projects per period; and (d) elapsed time for completed projects, with unfinished work treated as ongoing/censored rather than silently omitted. Define whether an item reopened after completion is counted as one completed lifecycle, currently complete, or a new attempt; display the rule. Exclude paused, someday, and archived items from active denominators by default while allowing transparent filters. Keep denominator/count beside every percentage. Data created before reliable lifecycle timestamps should be marked unavailable, not retroactively reconstructed from current status.
